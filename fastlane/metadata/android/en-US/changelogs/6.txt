DB's live number is now believed only when it reports a delay. For
most trains DB restates the timetable, which is not an observation:
over seven collected days it called 99% of trains three hours out
"on time", and a quarter of those arrived more than two minutes late.
Expect the app to disagree with the platform display more often; the
prediction screen says why. Scored against DB's own forecasts, this
version comes out lower on arrival times and on missed connections.

The interface is now available in German.
