Opening a station board and going back no longer empties the From and
To fields, so a search can be rerun without retyping both stations; the
chosen date and time, the Deutschland-Ticket setting and scroll
positions survive the trip too. A train running a minute early now reads
-1 instead of +-1, and a delay under a minute reads as on time.
Searching a date beyond the live timetable is much faster: the delay
history each departure needs is now fetched alongside the others and
reused across the search instead of being fetched again for every place
it appears.
